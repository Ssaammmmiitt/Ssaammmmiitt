## About

### Full-Stack Developer · Computer Engineering Student · Exploring Applied AI

- ⌖ **Based in** · Kathmandu, Nepal
- ◎ **Studying** · Computer Engineering at Kathmandu University
- ↗ **Currently exploring** · Full-stack systems, machine learning & applied AI

I enjoy building things and figuring out how they work.

My work spans across the stack—from responsive interfaces and backend APIs to data-driven applications, machine learning models, and AI-powered workflows.

## Featured Work

### 🔥 Prometheus — Wildfire Risk Forecasting

A daily wildfire risk forecasting system for Nepal combining satellite observations, weather, vegetation, terrain, fire history, machine learning, and an interactive forecasting interface.

`Python` · `Machine Learning` · `LightGBM` · `FastAPI` · `React` · `Geospatial Data`

### 📈 NepAI — Data & AI for NEPSE

A data-driven platform for exploring Nepal's stock market through market data, technical indicators, portfolio insights, and AI-powered analysis.

`Full Stack` · `Data` · `AI` · `NEPSE`

### 🧠 NoteMerge — Organizing Knowledge with AI

An application exploring semantic similarity and topic clustering to transform fragmented notes into structured knowledge and reduce redundancy.

`NLP` · `Embeddings` · `Semantic Search` · `Clustering`
